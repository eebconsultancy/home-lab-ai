Privacy Policy

Home Lab AI, by EEB CONSULTANCY LLC

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EEB CONSULTANCY LLC ("we", "us", or "our") processes information when you use the Home Lab AI iOS application ("Home Lab AI" or "the app").

Home Lab AI provides two experiences: Personal Mode and Professional Mode. These modes have different account, storage, and data-processing requirements.

1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, Home Lab AI may process:

  • Room photographs selected or captured by you.
  • AI design instructions, staging briefs, and preferences.
  • AI-generated and refined images.
  • Property names, room information, and project metadata.
  • Professional account identifiers and authentication information.
  • Purchase status, Credit balances, and transaction records.
  • Technical information needed for security, diagnostics, and abuse prevention.
  • Product analytics information, as described below.

We process this information to provide the features you request, maintain the service, protect accounts, and prevent misuse.

2. Personal Mode

2.1 Room photos

When you select or capture a room photograph in Personal Mode, the app sends the image to our Cloudflare backend for processing through Google's Gemini API, together with the style and room type you chose. The image is used only for that request.

Personal Mode follows a local-first storage approach. Original room photos and design history that you choose to keep are stored locally on your device.

Our Personal Mode backend processes submitted photographs for the requested AI operation and does not maintain a permanent server-side photo library or design-history backup.

Deleting locally stored content removes the corresponding app-managed local copy. Copies exported to other apps, saved to Photos, or included in device backups are managed separately.

2.2 AI-generated images

Generated images are returned to your device. You may save them in your design history or export them using supported iOS features.

Images that you do not save are not intentionally retained as permanent Personal Mode design-history records.

3. Professional Mode

Professional Mode provides account-based tools for managing properties, rooms, staging briefs, generated images, and image versions.

Unlike Personal Mode, Professional Mode uses cloud infrastructure to support authenticated project management and AI processing.

3.1 Account information

Professional Mode uses Sign in with Apple.

When you sign in, the app does not ask Apple for your name or email address. We receive an Apple-provided account identifier. We store it only as a keyed hash, together with an encrypted Apple sign-in token that lets us revoke the sign-in when you delete your account. We do not store your name or email address.

We use account information to authenticate you, associate Professional projects with your account, maintain Credit records, and provide account-related services.

3.2 Property and room information

Professional Mode may process and store information associated with your projects, including:

  • Property and room names.
  • Room categories and descriptions.
  • Staging briefs and design preferences.
  • Selected or uploaded room photographs.
  • Generated staging images.
  • Multi-Angle image groups.
  • Refinement requests and image-version history.
  • Project status and related metadata.

This information is used to provide Professional features, maintain project continuity, and support the operations you request.

3.3 Photos, generated images, and cloud storage

In Professional Mode, your original room photographs stay on your device. Our server stores information about each photo (such as its size and a checksum) and the property and room information you enter.

When you request an AI operation, the app uploads a copy of the photograph needed for that operation. Location and camera metadata (EXIF, including GPS) must be removed before upload; uploads that contain it are rejected. An uploaded copy is deleted when the operation finishes and in any case within 24 hours.

A generated image is kept in temporary cloud storage until your device downloads it, and then deleted. A generated image that is not downloaded is deleted after 14 days. Your design instructions are kept only while the operation is waiting or running. Records of completed operations (without images) are kept for up to 90 days for support, refunds, and billing reconciliation.

4. Artificial intelligence processing

Home Lab AI uses Google's Gemini API to provide AI-powered interior design and virtual staging.

When you request an AI operation, the photographs, instructions, and other information necessary to complete that operation may be transmitted from the app to our backend and then to Google's Gemini API.

AI operations may include:

  • Room redesign and interior visualization.
  • Virtual furniture staging.
  • Multi-Angle image generation.
  • Image refinement.
  • Photo comparison and room-matching analysis.

AI processing takes place through external services and is not performed entirely on your device.

Home Lab AI uses the paid Gemini API service. Under Google's terms for paid services, Google does not use the photos, instructions, or generated images we send to improve its products, and processes them as our data processor. Google keeps prompts, contextual information, and responses for 55 days to detect and prevent abuse of its service, and may store them transiently or cache them in any country where Google or its agents have facilities.

AI-generated images may contain inaccuracies or visual inconsistencies and should not be treated as verified representations of physical spaces.

5. Analytics

Analytics in Home Lab AI is optional and off unless you choose "Share Analytics". You are asked once, at the end of the first-run questions, and you can change this preference at any time in Settings (in Personal Mode and in Professional Mode).

Home Lab AI does not use a third-party analytics service. If you choose "Share Analytics", the app sends a small number of usage events to our own analytics service, which runs on Cloudflare. Each event contains only an event name and, for some events, one value from a fixed list:

  • App opened, with the workspace (Personal or Professional) that is open.
  • First-run questions completed (without your answers or the workspace chosen).
  • Photo selected, with the source (camera or photo library).
  • AI generation started (new or regenerated), succeeded, or failed (with a general failure category such as network or server).
  • Subscription screen viewed (with where it was opened from), purchase started or completed (with the plan: weekly or yearly), restore result, and share.

Our analytics service adds each accepted event to a daily total and does not store the individual event. We do not store your IP address, a device or installation identifier, a user or account identifier, your name, your email address, or the time of the event (only the calendar day). Events from the same person are not linked to each other. Your IP address is processed briefly by Cloudflare to deliver the request and to apply rate limits, and is not written to our analytics database. To prevent abuse, our service counts requests per network for one minute using a one-way hash of the network part of your IP address; this count is kept only in memory and is not stored.

Your answers to the first-run questions (your role, how many homes or properties you work with, and your main goal) are stored only on your device and are used to set up the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, including when you choose "Share Analytics". We also do not send information that would reveal them, such as which workspace the first-run questions led you to or the subscription screen shown right after them.

  • Nothing is collected before you choose "Share Analytics", and events from before that choice are not sent later.
  • To limit traffic, the app keeps on your device only a count of events sent today and, if our service asks it to pause, the time to resume. These are not identifiers and are never sent.
  • If you turn analytics off, collection stops immediately and requests still in progress are cancelled. An event that our service already received stays in the daily total, because it cannot be traced back to you.
  • Choices made in earlier versions of the app (which used a third-party analytics service) are not treated as consent. Analytics stays off until you choose again.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers and do not request App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • We do not use fingerprinting, session replay, or screen recording.
  • Analytics events never contain room photographs, AI-generated images, design instructions, style or room names, product identifiers, or purchase transaction details.
  • Professional Mode screens do not send additional usage events; with "Share Analytics" on, the app reports only which workspace is open when the app starts.

Daily totals are kept for up to 400 days and then deleted automatically. Reports are available only to authorized staff, show totals rather than individual events, and hide small groups.

6. Authentication and security

We use security measures designed to protect Home Lab AI and prevent unauthorized use.

Depending on the feature, these measures may include Apple's App Attest and DeviceCheck, authenticated requests, request validation, and backend access controls.

Security processing may involve:

  • Device-integrity verification results.
  • Authentication identifiers.
  • Request identifiers and timestamps.
  • Rate-limit and abuse-prevention counters.
  • Records needed to prevent repeated promotional Credit claims.

Personal Mode does not have accounts. To limit abuse, our Personal Mode backend checks a DeviceCheck token with Apple (the token is not stored) and counts requests per salted hash of your IP address. These counters expire automatically: rate-limit counters after about 10 minutes and fair-use counters after at most 30 days.

To make sure the free Personal generation and the free Professional Welcome Credits are used only once per device, we use Apple's DeviceCheck service to set a per-device flag that Apple stores for us. The flag contains no personal information, is not linked to your account, and stays with the device even if you delete the app or your account.

Professional Mode may retain account-associated security and transaction records where necessary to protect the service and prevent fraud.

These measures are intended to protect the service but cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Purchases, subscriptions, and Credits

Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

We do not receive your complete payment-card details from Apple.

Personal Mode may offer subscriptions. Professional Mode may offer Credit packages used for eligible AI operations.

Personal Mode subscription status is checked on your device through Apple's StoreKit. Our Personal Mode backend does not receive purchase information. For Professional Credits, our backend verifies Apple's signed transaction and keeps the records listed below with your Professional account.

To provide purchased features, we may process:

  • Apple transaction identifiers.
  • Purchased product identifiers.
  • Purchase and entitlement status.
  • Credit balances and transaction history.
  • Credit reservations, deductions, and refunds.
  • Records necessary to prevent duplicate transactions.

These records may be retained as necessary for accounting, fraud prevention, purchase reconciliation, and applicable legal obligations.

8. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested features, maintain security, resolve technical or purchase issues, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

8.1 Personal Mode

Personal Mode design history is stored on your device. Our backend does not keep your photos or generated images after the request is complete. Abuse counters expire as described in section 6. Our Personal Mode backend processes operational records used for security and troubleshooting, such as request outcomes. These records are not intended to contain photographs or generated images. Cloudflare may process technical request metadata, including IP addresses, in its infrastructure logs. Retention of provider-managed logs depends on the applicable Cloudflare configuration and service terms; you may contact us for further information.

8.2 Professional Mode

Professional Mode retains account information, property and room information, and photo information for as long as your account exists or until you delete them. Uploaded photo copies are deleted within 24 hours, and generated images are deleted when your device has downloaded them or after 14 days. Records of completed AI operations (without images) are kept for up to 90 days. Download the generated images you want to keep; the app saves them on your device.

8.3 Security and transaction records

Security, purchase, and accounting records are retained only while needed for service security, transaction reconciliation, dispute resolution, or applicable legal obligations. Where a fixed retention period is not stated, we review the continued need for the information and delete or de-identify it when that need ends, unless retention is required by law.

Optional analytics totals are kept for up to 400 days.

We do not retain all categories of information indefinitely.

9. Account and data deletion

Professional users can request account deletion through the account-management features provided in the app.

Account deletion initiates the applicable deletion or de-identification procedures for associated account and project information.

Account-associated projects and information are deleted or de-identified through the account-deletion process, except where limited records must be retained for accounting, transaction disputes, security, or legal compliance. Unused Credits attached to a deleted Professional account will no longer be available through that account. Before confirming deletion, the app warns about this consequence. Contact us before deletion if you have unused purchased Credits and need assistance; applicable statutory rights remain unaffected.

Deleting the app from your device does not automatically delete an existing Professional account or cancel an App Store subscription.

To request assistance with account deletion or personal information, contact info@eebconsultancyllc.com.

Our Personal Mode backend runs close to Google Cloud in the United States to reach the Gemini API. Our optional analytics totals are stored in a Cloudflare database located in the Asia-Pacific region. Photos and instructions sent for AI processing are processed by Google, which may store them transiently in any country where it has facilities.

Where required by applicable law, we use appropriate contractual and organizational safeguards for transfers of personal information between countries. The applicable safeguards depend on the service provider, origin and destination of the transfer, and relevant law. Contact us if you need information about safeguards applicable to your data.
