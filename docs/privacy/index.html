Privacy Policy

Home Lab AI, by EEB CONSULTANCY LLC

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EEB CONSULTANCY LLC ("we", "us", or "our") processes information when you use the Home Lab AI iOS application ("Home Lab AI" or "the app").

Home Lab AI provides two experiences: Personal Mode and Professional Mode. These modes have different account, storage, and data-processing requirements.

1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, Home Lab AI may process:

  • Room photographs selected or captured by you.
  • AI design instructions, staging briefs, and preferences.
  • AI-generated and refined images.
  • Property names, room information, and project metadata.
  • Professional account identifiers and authentication information.
  • Purchase status, Credit balances, and transaction records.
  • Technical information needed for security, diagnostics, and abuse prevention.
  • Product analytics information, as described below.

We process this information to provide the features you request, maintain the service, protect accounts, and prevent misuse.

2. Personal Mode

2.1 Room photos

When you select or capture a room photograph in Personal Mode, the app sends the image to our Cloudflare backend for processing through Google's Gemini API.

Personal Mode follows a local-first storage approach. Original room photos and design history that you choose to keep are stored locally on your device.

Our Personal Mode backend processes submitted photographs for the requested AI operation and does not maintain a permanent server-side photo library or design-history backup.

Deleting locally stored content removes the corresponding app-managed local copy. Copies exported to other apps, saved to Photos, or included in device backups are managed separately.

2.2 AI-generated images

Generated images are returned to your device. You may save them in your design history or export them using supported iOS features.

Images that you do not save are not intentionally retained as permanent Personal Mode design-history records.

3. Professional Mode

Professional Mode provides account-based tools for managing properties, rooms, staging briefs, generated images, and image versions.

Unlike Personal Mode, Professional Mode uses cloud infrastructure to support authenticated project management and AI processing.

3.1 Account information

Professional Mode uses Sign in with Apple.

When you authenticate, we may receive an Apple-provided account identifier and, depending on the information you authorize Apple to share, your name or email address.

If you use Apple's Hide My Email feature, Apple may provide a private relay email address.

We use account information to authenticate you, associate Professional projects with your account, maintain Credit records, and provide account-related services.

3.2 Property and room information

Professional Mode may process and store information associated with your projects, including:

  • Property and room names.
  • Room categories and descriptions.
  • Staging briefs and design preferences.
  • Selected or uploaded room photographs.
  • Generated staging images.
  • Multi-Angle image groups.
  • Refinement requests and image-version history.
  • Project status and related metadata.

This information is used to provide Professional features, maintain project continuity, and support the operations you request.

3.3 Cloud storage

Professional Mode may store uploaded photographs, generated images, and associated project information in cloud infrastructure.

Cloud storage is different from the local-first storage used by Personal Mode.

Stored content may remain associated with your Professional account until it is deleted under the applicable feature rules, account-deletion procedures, or retention policies.

Temporary image files, project records, account information, and transaction records may have different retention periods.

4. Artificial intelligence processing

Home Lab AI uses Google's Gemini API to provide AI-powered interior design and virtual staging.

When you request an AI operation, the photographs, instructions, and other information necessary to complete that operation may be transmitted from the app to our backend and then to Google's Gemini API.

AI operations may include:

  • Room redesign and interior visualization.
  • Virtual furniture staging.
  • Multi-Angle image generation.
  • Image refinement.
  • Photo comparison and room-matching analysis.

AI processing takes place through external services and is not performed entirely on your device.

Google processes submitted content according to the terms and data-processing practices applicable to the Gemini API service used by Home Lab AI.

AI-generated images may contain inaccuracies or visual inconsistencies and should not be treated as verified representations of physical spaces.

5. Analytics

Home Lab AI uses Amplitude to understand how the app is used and improve its functionality.

Analytics may include information such as screen interactions, feature usage, and whether an AI generation was requested or completed.

  • We do not use advertising identifiers for targeted advertising.
  • We do not request App Tracking Transparency permission for advertising tracking.
  • We do not use Amplitude Session Replay or screen-recording analytics.
  • We do not intentionally include room photographs or AI-generated images in analytics events.
  • Personal Mode analytics use an anonymous installation-level identifier rather than your name or Apple ID.

Technical information may be processed by analytics services according to their applicable service configuration and privacy practices.

6. Authentication and security

We use security measures designed to protect Home Lab AI and prevent unauthorized use.

Depending on the feature, these measures may include Apple's App Attest and DeviceCheck, authenticated requests, request validation, and backend access controls.

Security processing may involve:

  • Device-integrity verification results.
  • Authentication identifiers.
  • Request identifiers and timestamps.
  • Rate-limit and abuse-prevention counters.
  • Records needed to prevent repeated promotional Credit claims.

Personal Mode may use salted or hashed identifiers for rate limiting and fair-use enforcement.

Professional Mode may retain account-associated security and transaction records where necessary to protect the service and prevent fraud.

These measures are intended to protect the service but cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Purchases, subscriptions, and Credits

Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

We do not receive your complete payment-card details from Apple.

Personal Mode may offer subscriptions. Professional Mode may offer Credit packages used for eligible AI operations.

To provide purchased features, we may process:

  • Apple transaction identifiers.
  • Purchased product identifiers.
  • Purchase and entitlement status.
  • Credit balances and transaction history.
  • Credit reservations, deductions, and refunds.
  • Records necessary to prevent duplicate transactions.

These records may be retained as necessary for accounting, fraud prevention, purchase reconciliation, and applicable legal obligations.

8. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested features, maintain security, resolve technical or purchase issues, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

8.1 Personal Mode

Personal Mode design history is primarily stored on your device. Our backend does not maintain a permanent server-side backup of your Personal Mode room-photo library.

8.2 Professional Mode

Professional Mode may retain account information, project metadata, uploaded images, generated results, and image-version records in cloud storage.

Certain temporary cloud-stored images may be subject to automatic retention and deletion periods, depending on the feature and account entitlement.

Where a 30-day cloud-retention period applies, the relevant files may be removed after that period. This does not mean every category of account or transaction information is deleted after 30 days.

Users should download important generated images they wish to retain independently of the app.

8.3 Security and transaction records

Security, purchase, and accounting records may be retained for different periods where reasonably necessary or legally required.

We do not retain all categories of information indefinitely.

9. Account and data deletion

Professional users can request account deletion through the account-management features provided in the app.

Account deletion initiates the applicable deletion or de-identification procedures for associated account and project information.

Some information may be retained where required or permitted for legal compliance, accounting, fraud prevention, or security.

Deleting the app from your device does not automatically delete an existing Professional account or cancel an App Store subscription.

To request assistance with account deletion or personal information, contact info@eebconsultancyllc.com.

11. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not operate an advertising network within Home Lab AI.
  • We do not use room photographs for targeted advertising.
  • We do not require access to your entire photo library when you select individual images.
  • We do not provide a public social network or public user-content feed.
  • We do not collect complete payment-card details.

12. International data processing

Our infrastructure and service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence.

Where required, international processing and transfers are subject to applicable legal requirements and safeguards.
