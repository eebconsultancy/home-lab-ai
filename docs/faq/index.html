Getting Started

What is Home Lab AI?

Home Lab AI is an AI-powered interior design and virtual staging app for iPhone and iPad.

It helps you visualize room transformations, explore interior styles, and create redesigned images from photographs of real spaces.

The app includes Personal Mode for individual room design and Professional Mode for property staging and project management.

How does Home Lab AI work?

Upload or capture a room photograph, select the available design options, and request an AI generation.

Home Lab AI securely sends the necessary information through its backend to Google's Gemini AI service. The generated image is then returned to the app.

What is the difference between Personal and Professional Mode?

Personal Mode

Designed for individual users who want to redesign rooms, explore interior styles, and save their favorite results.

Professional Mode

Designed for property owners, real estate professionals, interior designers, and staging professionals who need property and room management, virtual staging, Multi-Angle generation, and image refinement.

Do I need an account?

Personal Mode does not require a Professional account.

Professional Mode requires authentication using Sign in with Apple.

Does Home Lab AI require an internet connection?

Yes. AI generation and cloud-based Professional features require an internet connection.

Locally saved Personal Mode designs may remain available without an internet connection.

Personal Mode

How do I create a room design?

Open the Create tab, or select Interior Design from the Tools tab.

  1. Take a photograph or select one from your library.
  2. Choose a room type.
  3. Select a design style.
  4. Tap Generate.
  5. Review your AI-generated design.

Which room types are supported?

Personal Mode supports Auto Detect, Living Room, Bedroom, Kitchen, Bathroom, Dining Room, Home Office, Kids Room, and Outdoor/Balcony.

Auto Detect allows the AI to identify the room type from the selected photograph.

Which design styles are supported?

Personal Mode includes Modern, Scandinavian, Minimalist, Industrial, Luxury, and Vintage.

Can I save my generated designs?

Yes. Generated designs can be saved in your Personal Mode design history.

Saved Personal Mode history is stored locally on your device.

You can also export images to your Photos library or share them using the iOS share sheet.

Is Personal Mode free?

Personal Mode offers a limited free AI design experience.

Additional generations may require a Home Lab AI Premium subscription.

The app displays the applicable offer and subscription terms before purchase.

What does Home Lab AI Premium include?

Home Lab AI Premium provides access to eligible Personal Mode premium features, including additional AI design generations.

Available subscription plans and prices are displayed in the app and processed through Apple's App Store.

Does a Personal Mode subscription include Professional Credits?

No. Personal Mode subscriptions and Professional Mode Credits are separate products unless a specific offer explicitly states otherwise.

How do I restore a subscription?

Open Home Lab AI, go to Profile, and select Restore Purchases under Subscription.

Make sure you are using the Apple Account associated with the original purchase.

Professional Mode

What is Professional Mode?

Professional Mode provides an organized workspace for creating and managing AI-powered virtual staging projects.

It includes tools for managing properties, rooms, staging images, and design versions.

Who is Professional Mode designed for?

Professional Mode is intended for real estate agents, property managers, interior designers, home staging professionals, and other users who need structured virtual staging workflows.

How do I access Professional Mode?

Switch to Professional Mode within Home Lab AI and follow the Sign in with Apple authentication process.

Once authenticated, you can access your Professional workspace and available features.

What are Properties and Rooms?

A Property represents a location or project you want to stage.

Rooms organize the individual spaces within that property.

This structure helps you manage staging projects and their generated images.

What is virtual staging?

Virtual staging uses AI to visualize furniture, decor, and interior design changes in photographs of existing spaces.

It can help illustrate a room's potential without physically furnishing the property.

What is Multi-Angle generation?

Multi-Angle allows you to submit multiple photographs of the same room.

Home Lab AI analyzes the photographs and generates staging results intended to maintain visual consistency across different viewing angles.

You can use between two and six photographs for a supported Multi-Angle request.

Because results are AI-generated, exact consistency is not guaranteed.

What happens if one Multi-Angle image fails?

Successfully generated images remain available according to the operation's recorded results.

Credits are charged for successful images according to the applicable Credit rules. Failed images are handled separately.

What is Refine Image?

Refine Image lets you request changes to an existing AI-generated staging image.

For example, you can ask the AI to adjust furniture, colors, or other visual details.

AI refinement may also change details outside the specific requested area.

Can I download Professional images?

Yes. Professional Mode supports downloading or exporting eligible generated images.

We recommend saving important results outside the app for long-term access.

Professional Credits

What are Credits?

Credits are digital units used to request eligible AI operations in Professional Mode.

Your available balance is associated with your Professional account.

How many Credits does an image cost?

  • Standard image generation: 1 Credit.
  • Multi-Angle generation: 1 Credit per generated image.
  • First eligible successful refinement of an original image lineage: Free.
  • Additional eligible refinements: 0.5 Credit each.

The app displays the applicable Credit requirements before a chargeable operation.

Do new Professional accounts receive free Credits?

Eligible new Professional accounts may receive 2 complimentary introductory Credits.

Eligibility is subject to account and device-integrity verification, including anti-abuse checks.

Not every account or device is guaranteed to qualify for introductory Credits.

How do I purchase Credits?

Open the Professional Credit purchase interface and select an available package.

Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store using the prices displayed before confirmation.

Do Credits automatically renew?

Professional Credit packages are intended as individual purchases rather than automatically renewing subscriptions.

Personal Mode subscriptions are managed separately.

What happens if an AI generation fails?

Home Lab AI uses a Credit reservation and settlement system.

If an operation fails without a successful result, reserved Credits may be released or restored according to the recorded outcome.

Some interrupted requests may require additional reconciliation before the final Credit result is known.

Can I transfer Credits to another account?

Credits are associated with the Professional account that owns them.

Credit transfers between accounts are not currently supported.

Privacy and Data

What happens to my room photographs?

Room photographs are transmitted through our backend to Google's Gemini API when necessary to process an AI request.

Personal Mode uses local-first storage for saved design history.

Professional Mode may use cloud storage for account-associated projects, photographs, and generated images.

See our Privacy Policy for more information.

Are my photographs publicly visible?

Home Lab AI does not provide a public gallery or social network that automatically publishes your uploaded photographs.

You control when you export or share images through supported features.

How long are Professional images stored?

Storage duration depends on the relevant feature and account entitlement.

Some temporary cloud-stored images may be subject to a 30-day retention period.

Account information, project metadata, and transaction records may follow different retention rules.

Download important images you want to preserve independently.

Can I delete my Professional account?

Yes. Professional Mode provides an account-deletion option.

Account deletion initiates the applicable removal procedures for associated account and project information.

Some records may be retained where required for legal, accounting, or security purposes.

Does deleting my account cancel a subscription?

No. App Store subscriptions must be managed separately through your Apple Account subscription settings.

AI Results and Troubleshooting

Why do generated results vary?

AI generation is not deterministic.

Different requests may produce different furniture arrangements, materials, lighting, or other details, even when the original photograph is unchanged.

Are generated designs architecturally accurate?

No. AI-generated designs are visual concepts, not verified architectural or engineering plans.

Always verify measurements, structural details, materials, and applicable building requirements before making real-world changes.

Can I use generated images for real estate listings?

Generated images may be used for lawful professional purposes, subject to applicable rights and local regulations.

When publishing virtually staged property images, you are responsible for making any disclosures required by applicable advertising or real estate rules.

Why did my generation fail?

Possible causes include:

  • Internet connectivity problems.
  • Temporary AI service interruptions.
  • Unsupported or unsuitable input photographs.
  • Account authentication issues.
  • Credit balance limitations.
  • Temporary service or security limits.

Check your internet connection, confirm that you are signed in when using Professional Mode, and review any error message shown in the app.

If the issue continues, visit our Support page.
