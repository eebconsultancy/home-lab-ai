Frequently Asked Questions

Home Lab AI, by EEB CONSULTANCY LLC

How does Home Lab AI work?

You give the app a photo of a room, choose a room type and a design style, and Home Lab AI sends that to our backend, which asks Google's Gemini API to generate a redesigned version of the room in the style you picked. The result is sent back to your device.

How do I create a room design?

From the Tools tab, tap "Try It!" on Interior Design (or use the Create tab directly). Step 1: take a photo of your room or choose one from your library. Step 2: pick a room type and a style, then tap Generate. Your design appears when it's ready.

What room types are supported?

Auto Detect, Living Room, Bedroom, Kitchen, Bathroom, Dining Room, Home Office, Kids Room, and Outdoor/Balcony. Auto Detect lets the AI infer the room type from the photo itself instead of you specifying one.

What design styles are supported?

Modern, Scandinavian, Minimalist, Industrial, Luxury, and Vintage.

Why do results vary between generations?

Generated designs come from an AI model, so results can differ between attempts even from the exact same photo and style choice — that's inherent to how the underlying AI works, not a bug. If a result doesn't look right, try again, adjust the room type or style, or retake the photo in better lighting.

Can I save my generated images?

Yes. Every design you generate is added to your design history (visible in Profile), stored on your device. From a design's detail screen you can also export the image to your Photos library.

Can I share a design?

Yes — open a design and use the Share button to open the native iOS share sheet with the generated image, the same way you'd share any photo.

Is there a free way to try it?

Yes. Every install gets 1 free AI design before a Home Lab AI Premium subscription is required for further generations.

What does Home Lab AI Premium include?

Unlimited AI generations, sold as Weekly or Yearly auto-renewing subscriptions with no free trial, billed entirely through Apple's App Store. See our Terms of Use for the full subscription terms.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Home Lab AI > Profile, then tap Restore Purchases under Subscription. This is needed after reinstalling the app or on a new device signed into the same Apple ID.

What happens to my room photos?

Your photo is sent to our backend to be processed and is not stored there afterward — the backend does not keep a copy once it responds. Your original photo and any saved design history live locally on your device only. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Does Home Lab AI need an internet connection?

Yes — generating a design calls a remote AI service, so you need an active internet connection. Browsing your existing saved design history does not require one.

A generation failed — what should I check?

Most failures come down to connectivity: check your internet connection and try again. If it keeps failing, see our Support page for more troubleshooting steps and how to contact us.

How do I contact support?

Email info@eebconsultancyllc.com, or use the Feedback option in Profile > Support & Legal, which opens a prefilled email to the same address. See also our Support page.
