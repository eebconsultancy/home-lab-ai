Basic troubleshooting

  • The app can't generate a design. Check your internet connection, then try again. Generation calls a remote AI service, so a slow or unstable connection can cause a timeout.
  • A generated design didn't turn out the way you expected. AI-generated results can vary between attempts, even from the same photo and style. Try adjusting the room type or style, retaking the photo in better lighting, or generating again.
  • The app is showing content in the wrong language. Home Lab AI follows your device's language setting by default; you can also set a language directly in Profile > Language.
  • A saved design is missing. Your design history is stored on your device. Reinstalling the app, deleting its data, or switching to a new device will not carry old history over automatically.
  • The app won't let me generate another design. Free use is limited; unlimited generations require a Home Lab AI Premium subscription. See below if you believe you're subscribed but the app disagrees.

Purchases & subscription support

Home Lab AI Premium (Weekly or Yearly) is billed and managed entirely through your Apple ID via the App Store — we never see or store your payment details.

  • Restore a purchase (e.g. after reinstalling, or on a new device signed into the same Apple ID): open Home Lab AI > Profile > Restore Purchases.
  • View, change, or cancel your subscription: on your iPhone, open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Home Lab AI, or manage.apple.com/subscriptions.
  • Billing questions or a refund request: these are handled by Apple, not us — see Apple's subscription support.
