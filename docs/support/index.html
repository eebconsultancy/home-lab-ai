Getting Started

What is the difference between Personal and Professional Mode?

Personal Mode is designed for individual users who want to visualize interior design changes and explore different room styles.

Professional Mode provides additional tools for virtual staging, property management, room organization, Multi-Angle generation, and image refinement.

Professional Mode requires Sign in with Apple and uses a Credit-based system for eligible AI operations.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. AI generation requires an internet connection because images are processed through a remote AI service.

Some locally saved Personal Mode content may remain accessible offline.

Personal Mode Troubleshooting

  • The app cannot generate a design. Check your internet connection and try again. AI generation may fail during temporary service interruptions or network timeouts.
  • The generated design looks different from what I expected. AI results can vary between generations. Try selecting a different design style, adjusting the room type, or using a clearer photograph.
  • The app is displaying the wrong language. Home Lab AI supports multiple languages. Check your device language settings or open Profile > Language to select an available language.
  • My saved designs are missing. Personal Mode design history is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app or its data may permanently remove locally stored designs. Previously saved content may not automatically transfer to another device.
  • I cannot generate another design. Free generations are limited. Additional Personal Mode generations may require an active Home Lab AI Premium subscription.

Professional Mode Troubleshooting

I cannot sign in to Professional Mode

Professional Mode uses Sign in with Apple.

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Confirm that your Apple Account is available on your device.
  • Close and reopen the app if authentication becomes unresponsive.
  • Check whether Apple's authentication services are temporarily unavailable.

If the problem continues, contact support with a description of the error.

I cannot create a Property or Room

Make sure you are signed in to your Professional account and have an active internet connection.

Some operations may be subject to account or service limits.

If a Property or Room cannot be created, restart the app and try again.

My Professional project or image is missing

Professional projects and images may use account-associated cloud storage.

Confirm that you are signed in using the same Apple account originally associated with the project.

Some cloud-stored images may be subject to retention periods.

If a project or image unexpectedly disappears, contact support.

Multi-Angle generation is not working

Multi-Angle is designed for multiple photographs of the same room.

For supported requests, select between two and six room photographs.

For better results:

  • Use clear, well-lit photographs.
  • Photograph the same room from different angles.
  • Avoid mixing photographs from unrelated rooms.
  • Make sure the images are fully uploaded before starting generation.

The AI may reject photographs that cannot be reliably matched to the same room.

Why are Multi-Angle results inconsistent?

Multi-Angle generation attempts to maintain visual consistency between different views of a room.

However, AI-generated furniture, materials, proportions, and layouts may vary between images.

Exact visual or geometric consistency is not guaranteed.

Refine Image did not make the requested change

Image refinement uses AI to interpret your instructions.

The result may differ from your request, and some image details may change unexpectedly.

Try using a clear, specific instruction.

Before submitting another refinement, check the Credit requirement displayed in the app.

Professional Credits

How do Credits work?

Professional Mode uses Credits for eligible AI operations.

  • Standard image generation: 1 Credit.
  • Multi-Angle: 1 Credit per generated image.
  • First eligible successful refinement: Free.
  • Additional eligible refinements: 0.5 Credit each.

The applicable Credit cost is displayed before a chargeable operation.

I did not receive my introductory Credits

Eligible new Professional accounts may receive 2 complimentary Credits.

Eligibility depends on account and device-integrity verification.

Introductory Credits are not guaranteed for every account or device.

If you believe you are eligible but did not receive the introductory allocation, contact support.

My Credits were deducted but generation failed

Home Lab AI uses a Credit reservation and settlement system.

When an operation fails without a successful result, reserved Credits may be released or restored according to the recorded outcome.

Some interrupted operations require additional reconciliation before the final Credit balance is determined.

If your balance remains incorrect, contact support and include:

  • The approximate date and time of the operation.
  • The feature you were using.
  • The number of Credits involved.
  • Any error message displayed.

Please do not send passwords, authentication tokens, or payment-card information.

Can I restore purchased Credits?

Professional Credits are associated with your authenticated Professional account.

Sign in using the same account associated with the original purchase.

If purchased Credits are missing, contact support with the relevant purchase information.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Home Lab AI purchases are processed through Apple's App Store.

We do not receive or store your complete payment-card information.

Restore a Personal Mode subscription

Open Home Lab AI > Profile > Restore Purchases.

Use the Apple Account associated with the original subscription.

Manage or cancel a subscription

On your iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name.
  3. Select Subscriptions.
  4. Find Home Lab AI.
  5. Manage or cancel your subscription.

Deleting Home Lab AI does not automatically cancel an active App Store subscription.

Request a refund

Refund requests for App Store purchases are handled through Apple.

Visit Apple's Report a Problem to review available refund options.

For questions about missing Credits or incorrect purchase entitlements, you may also contact our support team.
